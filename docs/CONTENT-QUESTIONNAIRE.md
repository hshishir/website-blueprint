# Content Questionnaire

Complete this before replacing the sample content.

## Required company information

- Company name
- Short company name
- One-sentence company description
- Primary customer type
- Primary customer problem
- Main business outcome
- Geographic focus
- Contact email
- Primary call to action
- Secondary call to action

## Homepage positioning

Provide:

- Eyebrow: 3–8 words
- Headline: ideally 6–12 words
- Supporting paragraph: 25–50 words
- Three short hero cards:
  - Category
  - Outcome or value

## Services

For each service, provide:

- Service title
- Customer problem addressed
- What is delivered
- Expected outcome
- Supporting technologies only when relevant

Recommended: 3–6 services.

## Approach

Provide 3–5 delivery stages. For each:

- Stage number
- Stage title
- What happens
- What decision or deliverable results

## Results and proof

Use only verified facts.

Possible inputs:

- Quantified customer outcomes
- Number or category of completed engagements
- Platforms implemented
- Industries supported
- Leadership experience
- Patents
- Certifications
- Approved testimonials
- Approved customer logos

If verified outcomes are unavailable, use qualitative differentiators rather than invented metrics.

## About

Provide:

- Company origin
- Leadership background
- Areas of specialization
- Industries served
- How the firm works with clients
- Why the company is credible

## Contact

Provide:

- CTA headline
- 1–3 sentence invitation
- Email or scheduling link
- Optional phone number
- Optional location
- Optional capability-statement link

## Brand assets

- Logo: SVG preferred
- Favicon
- Approved company name spelling
- Optional team photos
- Optional customer logos
- Optional social links

## Design decisions

Default selections:

- Dark ks-site-inspired theme
- Electric lime primary accent
- Cyan secondary accent
- Moderate animation
- No stock photography required
- Abstract interface and typography-led visual design

Document any approved overrides.
